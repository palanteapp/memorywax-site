Help and FAQ

Quick answers, what the messages mean, and how to reach a person. Inside the app, the lifebuoy button at the top of each screen explains the screen you’re on.

Questions people ask

Do I need a lot of records, or any?

No. You can name songs with Listen, search albums by name, see who played on any record, and keep a Dream Crate of records you want, all without owning a collection.

Is Memory Wax free?

Yes. The app is free to use, and there are no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything about your collection works without one, and it’s kept on your devices and in your private iCloud. An account is only for adding friends. You sign in with Apple or an email link, with no password.

Where is my collection stored?

On your phone, and in your own private iCloud if sync is on (it is by default). It’s never uploaded to a Memory Wax server. See iCloud and backup.

What does Confirmed mean?

The etched matrix in the deadwax matched the runout listed for one pressing. Likely means the barcode or cover points to one pressing but nothing rules out another. Album only means we know the album, not the pressing. Tap “How we know” on a record to see the evidence.

Why is my record only “Album only”?

Barcodes, labels and typed searches find the release, not the pressing. Only the deadwax can name the pressing, and only with a Discogs token to compare against. Here’s how.

Does it work without a Discogs account?

Yes. Scanning, credits (from MusicBrainz), Listen, shelves and sessions don’t need Discogs. A free Discogs token adds deadwax matching, Discogs credits and importing your collection.

Does Memory Wax record me or my camera?

No. Camera frames are read on your phone and not kept. Listen turns the sound into a short fingerprint that Apple’s Shazam matches, only while you’ve tapped Listen, and keeps no recording.

Does it play music?

It plays albums through Apple Music, using the account on your phone, and needs a subscription for full songs. Memory Wax never stores or streams audio itself. It supports Apple Music only.

Can my friends see my shelves?

Not yet. Friends can see your profile today. Seeing each other’s shelves, public shelf pages and following curators are coming later. Prices and places will never be shown.

Are rooms live?

You can plan a room today: a night, a time and the records in order, with a calendar reminder. Live chat between phones is coming later.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, any time: Settings → “Download a backup” (everything, with photos) or “Export as CSV” (a spreadsheet). If Memory Wax ever shuts down, the app promises 90 days’ notice and a final update that keeps everything working.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → “Erase everything on this phone”. If you made a community account, Community account → “Delete my account” removes your username, profile and friends from the server for good. Download a backup first if you want one.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. Memory Wax is for iPhone and iPad.

What the messages mean

You seeWhat to do
“The camera isn’t available here.”Turn on the camera in iOS Settings → Memory Wax → Camera, or use “Type it”.
“Couldn’t read a barcode.”More light, hold flatter, or type the digits. Many older records have no barcode. Use Label or Deadwax.
“No match yet”The release isn’t in the public databases, or there’s no connection. Check the spelling, or type it yourself.
“Nothing readable yet” (deadwax)Rake light across the runout from one side with the phone low on the other. Guide.
“MusicBrainz is busy right now.”It’s a free service shared by many apps. Try again in a minute.
“Add your Discogs token…”Deadwax matching and import need a token. Guide.
“Couldn’t name it yet.” (Listen)Turn it up or move closer, then try again.
“Apple Music isn’t answering on this iPhone.”Open the Music app once and play anything for a second. If that doesn’t help, check Settings → your name → Media & Purchases, or restart the phone.
“Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app to sync.”Sign in to iCloud in iOS Settings. Your records are safe on the phone meanwhile.
“Your iCloud storage is full.”Free up iCloud space. Sync resumes on its own.
“That sign-in link expired” / “Open the link on the phone that asked for it.”Send a new email, and open it on the same phone.
