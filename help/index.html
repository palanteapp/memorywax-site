Help and contact

Memory Wax is coming soon to iPhone and iPad. For anything at all, write to a person.

Write to us

Questions, ideas, a problem with a record, or a request about your details: info@memorywax.app. We read every message.

Leave the email list

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Or write to us and we will remove you.

In the app

The lifebuoy button at the top of each screen explains the screen you’re on. To take your collection with you, use Settings → “Download a backup” (everything, with photos) or “Export as CSV” (a spreadsheet). To remove everything from your phone, use Settings → “Erase everything on this phone”. If you made a community account, Community account → “Delete my account” removes it.

How we handle your information is in the privacy policy.
