Safety

Community rules

Memory Wax is for people who love records and the people who made them. Everyone who loves music is welcome here, whether you own ten records or ten thousand.

Private until you say otherwise

  • A new account can’t be found by anyone. You turn on Let people find me if you want that, and even then someone must type your exact username. There’s no list to browse and no search by first letters.
  • Your photo is hidden from people who find you unless you turn on Show my photo to people who find me.
  • Your collection, prices and places never leave your phone and your own iCloud.
  • You can block anyone: it ends any friendship, and they can no longer find you or ask again. You can unfriend without it being announced.

What we won’t allow

  • Hate and harassment. Slurs, threats, bullying, or going after someone for who they are.
  • Sexual content and nudity, in a name, username, photo or answer.
  • Pretending to be someone. Another person, a business, a musician’s official page, or Memory Wax.
  • Spam and scams. Repeated friend requests, links, or offers nobody asked for.
  • Anything illegal, and anything that puts a child at risk.
  • Other people’s private details. Addresses, phone numbers, or anything shared without permission.
  • False reports. Whether about an etching or about a person.

The app screens usernames and the text of your profile for hateful and explicit words, and refuses them with a message. No filter is perfect, which is why you can also report.

If something’s wrong

  1. Open the person’s profile and tap Block if you want them gone from your view. It is instant.
  2. Tap Report and pick a reason. Add a note if it helps. They aren’t told who reported them.
  3. A person on our team gets the report by email and aims to look within 24 hours.
  4. We can dismiss it, ask for a change, or suspend the account. A suspended account is hidden from everyone and can’t add friends or invite.

If you think we got something wrong, or something isn’t fixed, write to info@memorywax.app. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first; we can’t watch the app in real time.

How reports are kept

A report holds the reason, your note and both usernames, and it is kept even if either person deletes their account, so evidence can’t be erased. Handled reports are removed after one year, or 90 days after either person deleted their account, whichever is sooner. The longer story is in the privacy policy and the terms.

Etching reports

When you tell us what etching is on your copy, it shows as a collector report with a count, never as the official record. One person counts once, and an account has to be a few days old before it counts. Please only report what you read.
