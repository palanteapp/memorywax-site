Join the beta

Memory Wax is in testing on iPhone and iPad. We’re letting people in a few at a time, through Apple’s TestFlight.

How to get in

  1. Write to usEmail info@memorywax.app with the subject “Memory Wax beta”. Tell us a little about your records: what you collect, roughly how many, whether you DJ.
  2. Get the TestFlight inviteWhen a place opens, Apple emails you an invite. Install the free TestFlight app from the App Store, then tap the invite.
  3. Install Memory WaxTestFlight installs the app and keeps it updated. Your collection stays on your phone and in your own iCloud.

What we ask of testers

  • Try it with your real records, especially the tricky ones: reissues, promos, white labels, records without barcodes.
  • Tell us when it names the wrong pressing, misses a credit, or confuses you. Screenshots help.
  • Expect rough edges. Back up from Settings before big changes, as the app suggests.

Got a friend already in? They can send you an invite from the app’s Community page, so you’re friends from your first day.
