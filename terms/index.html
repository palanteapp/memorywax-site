Terms of use

Version 1 · in effect from 20 October 2026. Short, and in plain words. Collectors, casual listeners, producers and engineers all get the same treatment here.

These terms are an agreement between you and Memory Wax (“we”, “us”) about the Memory Wax app and website. By making a community account you agree to them. If you only use the app on your own phone, without an account, using the app means you accept the parts that apply to that, such as “Our part and yours” and “Other people’s data”.

Who can use it

  • You must be 13 or older. The app asks you to confirm this when you make an account. If you are under the age of adulthood where you live, check with a parent or guardian first.
  • One person, one account. Keep your sign-in to yourself.
  • Be yourself, or a name you really go by (a DJ name is fine). Don’t pretend to be another person, a business, or Memory Wax, and don’t use names such as “support” or “admin”.

Your collection is yours

Your records, grades, notes, prices and photos stay on your phone and in your own iCloud. We don’t get them and we don’t claim any rights to them.

What you share, and what that lets us do

If you make an account, you put a few things on our server: a username, a name, a small photo, three short answers, and your friend requests and blocks. They are yours. You give us permission to store them and to show them to the people your settings allow (see Privacy), only so the app can work. The permission ends when you delete your account, apart from the report copies described under “Reports” below.

The rules

The community rules are part of these terms. In short: no hate, harassment, threats, sexual content or nudity, no pretending to be someone, no spam, nothing illegal, no one else’s private information, and no false reports. The rules apply to every part of your profile, including your name, username, photo and answers, and to any note you send with a report.

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people. The app screens usernames and profile text for hateful and explicit words, you can block and report anyone from their profile, and we act on reports.

Reports and what we do about them

  • A report reaches us by email with the reason, your note and both usernames. We aim to look at it within 24 hours.
  • We may dismiss it, remove content, or suspend the account. A suspended account is hidden from everyone else and can’t add friends, follow, or invite. We may do this without warning when the harm is serious.
  • If you think we got it wrong, write to info@memorywax.app from the email you signed in with and a person will look again.
  • The person you report is not told who reported them. We keep the report, with both usernames, even if either account is deleted, so a person can’t wipe the evidence by deleting their account. Handled reports are removed after one year, or 90 days after either person deleted their account, whichever is sooner.
  • Reporting in bad faith, or filing a stream of reports to harass someone, breaks the rules too.

Etching reports from collectors

You can tell us which runout etching you read on your own copy of a pressing. We show these as collector reports, with a count, never as the official record. Report only what you actually read. We may hide any report that looks wrong, and a report only counts once an account has been open for a few days. You can take all your reports back in the app.

Other people’s data

Catalogue details, credits and covers come from sources such as Discogs, MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive, which have their own licences. Memory Wax is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Discogs. Don’t use the app to copy those sources in bulk.

Our part and yours

  • Memory Wax is provided as it is. We work hard to name pressings correctly, but a match can be wrong and a grade or price is a guide, not advice. Check against your own copy.
  • You are responsible for keeping a backup of your collection. We give you tools for it, but a phone can be lost.
  • To the extent the law allows, we aren’t liable for indirect losses, and our total responsibility to you is limited to what you paid us for the app, which today is nothing. Nothing here limits rights that the law says can’t be limited.

Ending things

You can stop at any time: Community account → “Delete my account”, or just delete the app. We can suspend or close an account that breaks these terms. If we ever shut the app down, we promise 90 days’ notice and a final update that keeps your collection and backups working.

Apple and its subsidiaries are third-party beneficiaries of these terms and may enforce them against you. Apple’s own terms for your device and Apple Account also apply.

Changes

We may change these terms. If a change matters, the app will tell you and ask you to agree again before you keep using Community. Each version has a number; this is version 1.
