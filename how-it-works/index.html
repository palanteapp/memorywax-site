How it works

From the record in your hands to the people who made it

Memory Wax is built on one idea: the record in your hands is a specific thing, made by specific people, and worth remembering properly.

  1. Add itScan the barcode, the center label, the deadwax or the cover, let Listen name a song, or just type it in. Already keep a list? Import your Discogs collection or a CSV. More on adding
  2. Know which pressingMemory Wax shows its evidence and labels the match Confirmed, Likely or Album only. When it can’t tell, it says so and lets you choose. It never guesses quietly.
  3. See who made itThe players, the producer, the engineer, the studio. Every name opens their page, and the other records they played on. The record page
  4. Put it onTap Listen and Memory Wax names the song playing in the room, tells you where the needle is, and keeps a session of the night. Listen · Sessions
  5. Keep it, share what you chooseGroup records on shelves, keep the ones on your wish list in your Dream Crate, make playlists, share a card. Prices and places stay private. Your collection

Why the evidence matters

Two records, one cover

A reissue and an original can share artwork, title and even a barcode. What tells them apart is small: a catalog number, a country, a few characters etched near the label. Memory Wax reads those clues and shows how it decided.

That’s why every record shows “How we know”, and why the app is happy to say “Album only” when that’s all it can honestly claim.

Where the information comes from

Open data, with credit

MusicBrainzThe open music database: releases, barcodes, credits, versions.
Cover Art ArchiveCover images.
Memory Wax’s record indexOur own copy of Discogs’s open data (CC0): pressings, runouts and credits, so deadwax matching works without a token. Etchings that collectors report are kept apart and labelled.
DiscogsYour own token is only for importing your own collection.
Apple Music and ShazamFinding albums, naming songs, playing and saving playlists, through the account already on your phone.
WikipediaShort biographies and album stories, shortened and credited.

Memory Wax is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Discogs. Liner notes are copyrighted, so the app links to them instead of copying them. Covers are shown from the Cover Art Archive to help you recognise a record.

What stays with you

Your collection is yours

It lives on your phone and in your own private iCloud. There are no ads and no tracking. The camera picture never leaves your phone. Prices and places never go to anyone else.
