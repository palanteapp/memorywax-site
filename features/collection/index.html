Features / Your collection

A collection you can search by who played on it

All your records in one place, with shelves you make yourself, a Dream Crate for the ones you want, and playlists from your own records.

All records

A grid or a list, with a status dot on each record showing how sure the match is. Search looks through titles, artists, labels, matrix numbers, your own memory notes, years and the names of people credited. When something matches, the row says why: “matched credit: Ron Carter”.

Filter by genre (genres come from Discogs imports), by 45s, or by Confirmed only. Sort by recent, A to Z, or year. Use Select to remove several at once. Removing always asks first, and a backup you saved earlier still has the record.

Records that aren’t Confirmed yet get a gentle nudge: “3 could be confirmed. Scan their deadwax to name the exact pressing.”

Shelves

A shelf is a group you make: “Spiritual jazz”, “Late night house”, “Gifts”. A record can sit on as many shelves as you like. Every shelf has its own audience setting: Just me, Friends, or Everyone. New shelves start as Just me.

You can play a shelf in order in the Apple Music player, or turn it into a playlist. Deleting a shelf never deletes the records.

Coming later: shelves that friends can actually see, and public shelf pages on memorywax.app. Today your shelves are visible only to you, whatever the setting says, and the app labels that plainly. The setting is there so your choices are ready when sharing opens.

Whatever happens later, prices, places and your location are never shown on a shelf.

Dream Crate

“Records you’d love to have, kept in one place.” Add a record from its page, from a person’s page (“+ Dream Crate” next to any album they played on), or straight from the Listen results. It’s a wishlist that starts from the music rather than from a store. Trading and for-sale listings between collectors, and alerts when a copy turns up, are coming later. Nothing is listed or sold today.

Playlists

Pick records from your collection and as many tracks from each as you like, name the playlist, add a line about it. Play or shuffle it in the Apple Music player, or save it to your Apple Music library. Playlists are private until you decide otherwise.

Yours to take with you

Export any time

“Export as CSV” makes a spreadsheet of your whole collection: title, artist, year, label, catalog, country, format, barcode, matrix, condition, price paid, where, your memory, shelves, and links to Discogs and MusicBrainz. Your records are always yours.
