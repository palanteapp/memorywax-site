Features / Your year

The turntable, counted

What you played most, who you heard most, and what you bought and never played. By December it’s your year in records.

What counts

A spin is counted when Listen names a song from a record on your shelf, or when you tap “I played this” on your copy. That’s all. Nothing is guessed, and nothing is sent anywhere. The counting happens on your phone.

What you see

  • The numbers: spins, records, listening sessions.
  • Played most: your top five records, with how many times each.
  • The player you heard most: the side player who turns up across your spins, such as “Ron Carter, bass, on 14 spins across 3 records”, and the next few after them.
  • Quick facts: your most-played artist, your busiest month, and about how much time Listen heard.
  • New this year, not played yet: records you added this year that haven’t seen the needle.
  • Your last eight listening sessions, each one a tap away.

Share your year

“Share your year” makes a 1080 × 1350 card, “2026 on the turntable”, with your spins, your top five and who you heard most, and your name and photo if you’ve set them. It goes out through the iOS share sheet with a caption you can edit.

If you haven’t played anything yet, the page says so and offers to start Listen. It never makes up numbers.
