Features / Rooms

The same album, at the same time

A room is a night you plan: the records, the order, the time. Everyone plays their own copy, or their own streaming, wherever they are.

Where this stands. You can plan a room today. Live chat between phones, public room pages and sending rooms to friends are coming later. Rooms are shown in the app as plans, not live events, and the page you see in the beta says so.

Plan a night in three steps

  1. The nightName it, pick a date and time, and say whether people will be in person or online. For an online night, each guest sees the time in their own time zone. For an in-person night, add the venue or neighborhood. Never post a home address; share the exact spot privately with the people you invite.
  2. The recordsPick the records you’ll play, in order, and add a line on why you chose each one if you like.
  3. Share itChoose who can find it, then list it.

What you get today

  • A room card with the name, time, place and records.
  • “Add to calendar”: a calendar file with a 15-minute reminder, 90 minutes long.
  • A countdown, and “Get your needle ready” when it’s time.
  • The track list to follow on your own turntable, with a note when it’s time to flip a side.
  • “Play the records” in the Apple Music player, if you’re streaming that night.

How it will work

Online rooms will have live chat for everyone listening, with host tools to slow the chat, pause it or end the room, and links turned off. Guests will open a room page in a browser, with or without the app. Friends-only rooms will appear on your friends’ Community pages. None of that connects phones yet.

About the music. Memory Wax doesn’t stream or broadcast anything in a room. Each person plays their own record or their own streaming account. If a night is held in a bar or shop, the venue handles its own music licensing.
