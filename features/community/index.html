Features / Community

People you know, on your terms

A profile, friends you add with a link or a username, and nothing public unless you decide.

Your name and photo

Your name (or DJ name) and photo go on the cards you share. Three short answers sit under your name on the Community page only: Where are you from? What do you spin? Favorite album? Your shared cards carry just your name.

An account, only if you want friends

Everything else works without any account. To add friends you sign in, with Sign in with Apple or with your email. For email, Memory Wax sends a link; tap it on the phone you asked from and you’re in. There are no passwords either way. Your email is only for signing in. It never shows on your profile.

Then you pick a username (3 to 20 letters, numbers, dots or underscores). Your name, photo and three answers are kept on a Memory Wax server so friends can see them. Your collection is never sent there.

Friends

  • Invite a friend. One tap makes a link, good for one use and for 14 days, and opens the share sheet. Your friend taps it, the app opens, and they’re one tap from being your friend.
  • Or find someone by their exact username. You’re private by default, so nobody can find you until you turn on “Let people find me”. Even then someone has to type your whole username: there’s no list to browse and no search by first letters. A search shows a username and name, and a photo only if its owner allows it. Someone’s three answers show only once you’re friends, or when they’ve asked you.
  • Both people say yes. A request is a request: they can accept, decline without you being told, and you can take it back.
  • Unfriend, block, report. Unfriending isn’t announced. Blocking ends any friendship and stops them finding you or asking again. Reports take a reason and an optional note, reach a person by email, and the person reported isn’t told who sent it. See the community rules.

Rules everyone agrees to

Before you make a profile the app asks you to confirm you’re 13 or older and agree to the Terms of Use and the community rules. Usernames and profile text are screened for hateful and explicit words, and a name that pretends to be Memory Wax or its staff is refused.

Your data, your call

Sign out any time and your collection stays. “Delete my account” removes your username, profile and friends from the server for good. And “Erase everything on this phone” also signs you out.

Coming later

Seeing each other’s shelves

The plan is that friends see the shelves you set to Friends, and that Everyone shelves get a public page anyone can open without the app. Both need server work that isn’t done yet, so today friends see your profile and nothing else. Following curators, shops and listening bars whose taste you admire comes after that. Memory Wax will tell you when each one opens.
