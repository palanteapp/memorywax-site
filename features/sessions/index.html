Features / Sessions

Your listening nights, kept

Leave Follow along on and every song Listen names lands in a session, in order. When the night’s over, you have the whole set.

How a session starts and ends

The first song Listen names starts a session. Each song after that joins it. If three hours pass without a named song, or you tap “Start a new session”, the next song starts a fresh one. Hearing the same song twice in a row is logged once. The app keeps your latest 400 sessions.

What’s in one

  • A title you choose (“DJ set at Lena’s”), or one the app makes, like “Saturday evening”.
  • The date, the start time, and the numbers: records, songs, listening time.
  • “What you played”: every record with how many songs, and whether it’s on your shelf.
  • “Who you heard”: the side players across those records, and who you heard most, such as “Ron Carter on bass, on 3 of the records”. This appears once the records’ credits have loaded.

Share it three ways

  • The card: a 1080 × 1350 image with the session name, the song count, the players you heard most and every song in order. Your photo and “Played by” line appear if you’ve set them.
  • The song list: text with a numbered list, each song with an Apple Music link so friends can play it.
  • An Apple Music playlist: saved to your library as “Session name, date”, “Played by you, N records on the turntable”. It’s private to you until you share it from the Music app.

Where to find them

On the Community page under “Your sessions”, in Your year, and from the Listen sheet (“This session so far”). Deleting a session removes it from your log. The spins already counted on your records stay.

Only you see a session unless you share it.
