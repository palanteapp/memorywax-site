Features / iCloud and backup

Your phone, your iPad, your files

Your collection lives on your devices and in your own private iCloud. No Memory Wax account is needed to keep it safe.

iCloud sync

Turn on “Sync with iCloud” (it’s on by default) and your iPhone and iPad stay the same. Memory Wax stores one private record in your iCloud. Only your Apple Account can read it. It holds:

  • your records, with their details, evidence and credits;
  • your copies: grades, price paid, where, plan, asking price, spins;
  • shelves, Dream Crate, playlists and rooms;
  • listening sessions;
  • your name, photo and three answers;
  • the covers you photographed yourself.

What stays on each device: photos of your copies, your Discogs token, your community sign-in, and settings such as Larger text and Reduce motion.

It syncs when you open the app, a few seconds after any change, whenever you come back to it, and every minute while it’s open. When the other device changed something you see “Updated from your other device.”

If two devices disagree

  • Changes from both are combined field by field.
  • If you changed the same thing on both, the device doing the syncing keeps its own version.
  • Removing wins: a record you removed on one device stays removed.
  • The same record added on both becomes one record.
  • If your other device removed a lot of records at once (at least 5 and more than 30% of your collection), Memory Wax asks first: “Remove them on this device too? Tap Cancel to keep them everywhere.”

The status line in Settings tells you what’s happening in plain words: “On · synced 3 min ago”, “Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app to sync”, “Your iCloud storage is full, so syncing is paused”. Turning sync off doesn’t delete the iCloud copy.

A backup file you keep

“Download a backup” makes one file that holds your records, shelves, grades, prices, Dream Crate, playlists, rooms, sessions, your name and photo, and photos of your copies and covers. Save it to iCloud Drive or anywhere. “Restore a backup” puts it back, after asking you to confirm that it replaces what’s on the phone.

Because it holds prices and places you paid, treat the backup file like any private document.

The safety nets

  • The Memory Wax folder. A few seconds after changes, the app saves a copy of your collection (no photos) in the Files app, under On My iPhone, Memory Wax. iCloud Backup includes that folder. If the app ever finds itself empty but the folder has a copy, it offers to put it back.
  • Before an update. When an update changes the app, a copy of what was on the phone is kept. If something looks wrong, Settings offers “Go back to before the last update”.
  • A backup reminder, if you have at least three records and haven’t saved a file in a month.

Take it with you

“Export as CSV” makes a spreadsheet of your whole collection. You can import it back, or into anything else.

If we ever close

90 days’ notice, and a final update

The app promises 90 days’ notice and a final update that keeps everything working, so your collection and your backups stay usable.
