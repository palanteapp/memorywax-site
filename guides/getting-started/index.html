Getting started

Ten minutes from install to a collection that knows who played on it.

  1. Install and openMemory Wax is in testing through Apple’s TestFlight. Ask to join, accept the invite, and install. The first time you tap “Start digging”, a short welcome asks where you’d like to start. You don’t need a record collection to begin.
  2. Add your first recordPick whichever fits what you have:
    • It has a barcode: scan it.
    • It’s older, or has no barcode: switch to Label and frame the center label, or tap Type it and enter the artist and title.
    • You already keep a list: import from Discogs, or upload a CSV from Settings.
    The first time you use the camera, your phone asks permission. The picture stays on your phone.
  3. Open the record pageTap the record. See who played on it, the producer, the engineer. Tap any name to see what else they worked on. Under “How we know” you can see exactly why the app believes what it does.
  4. Name the exact pressing (optional)Records start as Album only, or Likely. To go further, read the deadwax.
  5. Put a record onTap Listen on the Add tab, hold the phone near the speaker, and Memory Wax names the song and finds it on your shelf. More on sessions.
  6. Make it yoursAdd a grade and a memory in My Copy. Put records on a shelf with the + button. Tell Settings your name and photo for the cards you share.
  7. Keep it safeiCloud sync is on by default. It’s still worth tapping Settings → “Download a backup” once, and saving the file to iCloud Drive. The app reminds you if a month goes by.
Lost at any point? Tap the lifebuoy button at the top of the screen. It explains the screen you’re on.
