Guides / Add a record

Four ways to add a record

Each way reads a different part of the record, and each needs the phone held a little differently. These are the same pictures the app shows you, drawn for vinyl: where to look, how far to hold the phone, and where the light should come from.

WayBest forWhat you get
BarcodeRecords from the 1980s on, and most reissuesThe release, with a short list to choose from when several pressings share a number
Label45s and older records with no barcodeThe release, found by its catalog number. The deadwax can confirm the pressing after
DeadwaxNaming the exact pressingConfirmed when the etching matches a runout listed for one pressing
CoverA quick start, or a sleeve with no barcodeThe album. The exact pressing stays unconfirmed until you read the label or deadwax

1. Barcode

Look on the back cover or the inner sleeve. Most records after 1980 have one.

  1. Six inches, levelHold the phone about 6 inches away, level with the barcode.
  2. Bars inside the frameLine the bars up inside the frame. It reads on its own.
  3. LightDim light? Move near a window or lamp. No barcode? Tap Type it.

The full barcode guide →

2. Label

The paper circle in the middle of the record, with the label name and a catalog number.

  1. Fill the circleFill the circle in the frame and keep the phone flat above it.
  2. Look for the catalog numberIt’s printed on the label, like BLP 1577.
  3. Mind the glareTurn the record to avoid glare on the paper. Keep steady until it locks.

3. Deadwax

The smooth ring between the last groove and the label. The matrix is etched into it, and it’s the one mark that can tell one pressing from another.

  1. Six to eight inchesHold the phone 6 to 8 inches away.
  2. Tilt about 30°So a lamp or window skims across the etching. Shallow marks only show in raking light.
  3. Keep steadyHold still until it locks. Don’t cover the ring with your thumb.

The full deadwax guide →

4. Cover

The front of the sleeve, with the album title and artist.

  1. Fill the frameFill the frame with the front cover, square on.
  2. Tilt away from lightTilt away from lamps and windows so there’s no glare.
  3. Clear words work bestPlain cover? Try Barcode or Label instead.
What leaves your phone: the camera picture never does. Only the words or numbers read from it, such as a barcode or a catalog number, are sent to look the record up.
