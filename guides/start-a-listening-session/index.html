Guides / Listening sessions

Start a listening session

Put a record on, tap Listen, and let Memory Wax follow along. By the end of the night you have the whole set.

  1. Open ListenTap the Listen pill on the Add tab, or “What’s playing?” on Collection. The first time, your phone asks for the microphone. It only listens while this sheet is open.
  2. Turn on “Follow along”Tick the box: “name each track as it plays, and tell me when it’s time to flip”.
  3. Start listeningTap “Start listening” with the phone near the speaker. Each attempt lasts up to 15 seconds.
  4. Keep the sheet openMemory Wax times the next listen for when the song should end. You’ll see “Following along · next listen in about 2:40”. When a side should be over you get a tap and “Time to flip.” Closing the sheet stops following.
  5. Let it workEach named song joins the session. If it’s on a record you own, that record gets one spin. If it isn’t, you can add it from the same screen.
  6. FinishTap “Start a new session” when the night ends. Otherwise the next named song after three quiet hours starts a new one on its own.

What to do with the night

Open it from the Community page (“Your sessions”). Then:

  • Name it. “DJ set at Lena’s”. It saves when you leave the field.
  • Share the card. A 1080 × 1350 image with every song, in order.
  • Share the song list. Text with an Apple Music link for each song, so friends can play them.
  • Save it as an Apple Music playlist. It lands in your library, private to you. This needs Apple Music access and being signed in to Apple Music on the phone.

Tips and troubleshooting

  • “Couldn’t name it yet.” Turn it up a little or move closer. Quiet passages and live or rare records may not be in Shazam’s catalog.
  • “Memory Wax needs the microphone.” Turn it on in iOS Settings → Memory Wax → Microphone.
  • Shazam names a remaster, but you have the original. Memory Wax checks the songs against your pressing’s tracklist and can offer “Find my version”.
  • Listen works only in the iPhone and iPad app, not in a web browser.
Only you see a session unless you share it. The app keeps your latest 400 sessions.
